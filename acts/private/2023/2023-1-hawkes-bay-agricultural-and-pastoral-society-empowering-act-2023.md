# Hawke’s Bay Agricultural and Pastoral Society Empowering Act 2023

## Preamble

**(1)** The Hawke’s Bay Agricultural and Pastoral Society (the **Society**) owns fee simple interests in the Tomoana Showgrounds:

**(2)** The Society wishes to sell its interests in the Tomoana Showgrounds, and any other land it may acquire (the **land**), without being required to invest the money received from the sale to purchase other land suitable for the purposes of the Society as required by the Agricultural and Pastoral Societies Act 1908:

**(3)** The object of this Act is to remove the restrictions relating to the disposal of the land and to require the Society to apply the money received from the sale of the land towards purposes that are consistent with the objects of the Society:

**(4)** This object cannot be achieved without legislation:

The Parliament of New Zealand therefore enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the Hawke’s Bay Agricultural and Pastoral Society Empowering Act 2023.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## Part 1 Preliminary provisions

### 3 Purpose

The purpose of this Act is to—

(a) empower the Hawke’s Bay Agricultural and Pastoral Society (the **Society**) to sell its interests in the Tomoana Showgrounds, or any other land it may acquire, without being required to invest the money received from the sale in the purchase of other land suitable for the purposes of the Society; and

(b) require the Society to apply the money received from the sale of the land towards purposes that are consistent with the objects of the Society.

### 4 Interpretation

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,—

**land** means the land described in the Schedule or any other land acquired by the Society

**Society** means the Hawke’s Bay Agricultural and Pastoral Society.

## Part 2 Power to sell land

### 5 Power to sell land

**(1)** Despite anything in the Agricultural and Pastoral Societies Act 1908, the Society may, if the conditions in subsection (2) are satisfied, sell the land described in the Schedule, or any other land it may acquire, without having to invest the money received from the sale in the purchase of other land suitable for the purposes of the Society.

**(2)** The conditions are that—

(a) it appears advantageous to the Society to sell the land; and

(b) at least two-thirds of the members of the Society present at a general meeting of the Society have resolved to do so; and

(c) not less than 14 days’ notice of the general meeting and the proposal to sell the land has been given to members of the Society.

### 6 Certain enactments not to apply

The following enactments do not apply and must be treated as never having applied to the Society in relation to the land:

(a) sections 7 and 8(2) of the Agricultural and Pastoral Societies Act 1908:

(b) section 3 of the Agricultural and Pastoral Societies Amendment Act 1912:

(c) section 2 of the Agricultural and Pastoral Societies Amendment Act 1920.

### 7 Application of money received from sale

If the land is sold under section 5(1), the Society must apply the money received from the sale towards purposes that are consistent with the objects of the Society.
