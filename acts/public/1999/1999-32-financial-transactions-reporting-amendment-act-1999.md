# Financial Transactions Reporting Amendment Act 1999

An Act to amend the Financial Transactions Reporting Act 1996

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Financial Transactions Reporting Amendment Act 1999, and is part of the Financial Transactions Reporting Act 1996 (“the principal Act”).

## 2 Procedures for verifying identity

This section inserted s 12(3A) of the principal Act.

## 3 Obligation to keep verification records

This section substituted s 30(3) of the principal Act.
