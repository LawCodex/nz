# Government Superannuation Fund Amendment Act 1999

An Act to amend the Government Superannuation Fund Act 1956

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Government Superannuation Fund Amendment Act 1999, and is part of the Government Superannuation Fund Act 1956 (“the principal Act”).

## 2 Interpretation

This section substituted the definition of the term **Superintendent** in s 2(1) of the principal Act.

## 3 Superintendent of Government Superannuation Fund

This section inserted section 2C of the principal Act.
