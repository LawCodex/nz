# Arms Amendment Act 1999

An Act to amend the Arms Act 1983

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title and commencement

**(1) This Act may be cited as the Arms Amendment Act 1999, and is part of the Arms Act 1983 (“the principal Act”).** **(2) This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.**

## 2 New heading and sections inserted

This section inserted s 65A to 65H and the preceding heading of the principal Act.
