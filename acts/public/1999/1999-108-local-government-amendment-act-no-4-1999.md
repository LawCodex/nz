# Local Government Amendment Act (No 4) 1999

An Act to amend the Local Government Act 1974

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title and commencement

**(1) This Act may be cited as the Local Government Amendment Act (No 4) 1999, and is part of the Local Government Act 1974 (“the principal Act”).** **(2) This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.**
