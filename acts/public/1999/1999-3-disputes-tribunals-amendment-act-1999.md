# Disputes Tribunals Amendment Act 1999

An Act to amend the Disputes Tribunals Act 1988

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title and commencement

**(1) This Act may be cited as the Disputes Tribunals Amendment Act 1999, and is part of the Disputes Tribunals Act 1988 (“the principal Act”).** **(2) This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.**

## 2 Abandonment to bring claim within jurisdiction

This section amended s 14 of the principal Act.

## 3 Schedule 1 amended

This section amended Schedule 1 of the principal Act.
