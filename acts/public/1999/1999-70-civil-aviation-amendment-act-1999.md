# Civil Aviation Amendment Act 1999

An Act to—

(a) Amend the Civil Aviation Act 1990 to enable the implementation of certain Protocols to the Warsaw Convention relating to International Carriage by Air; and

(b) Provide for a system for controlling entry to airspace above the 1999-2000 America's Cup regatta

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title and commencement

**(1) This Act may be cited as the Civil Aviation Amendment Act 1999, and is part of the Civil Aviation Act 1990 (**the principal Act**).** **(2) Except as provided in section 2 and section 6, this Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.**

## Part 1 Amendments to principal Act

### 2 Commencement

This Part and the Schedule come into force on a date to be appointed by the Governor-General by Order in Council.

### 3 New Part 9A inserted

This section inserted Part 9A (comprising ss 91A to 91T) of the principal Act.

### 4 New Schedules inserted

The principal Act is amended by adding the Schedules 4 and 5 set out in the Schedule.

### 5 Repeals

The following enactments are repealed: (a) Part 1 and Schedules 1 and 2 of the Carriage by Air Act 1967:

(b) The Carriage by Air Amendment Act 1990:

(c) The Carriage by Air Amendment Act (No 2) 1990.
