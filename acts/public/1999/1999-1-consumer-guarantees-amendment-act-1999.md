# Consumer Guarantees Amendment Act 1999

An Act to amend the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title and commencement

**(1) This Act may be cited as the Consumer Guarantees Amendment Act 1999, and is part of the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993 (“the principal Act”).** **(2) This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.**

## 2 Jurisdiction

**(1) This section amended s 47 of the principal Act.** **(2) This section amended s 47(6) of the principal Act.**
