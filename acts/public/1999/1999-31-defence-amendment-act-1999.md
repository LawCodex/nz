# Defence Amendment Act 1999

An Act to amend The Defence Act 1990

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Defence Amendment Act 1999, and is part of The Defence Act 1990 (**the principal Act**).

## 2 Repeal of section 95

Section 95 of the principal Act is repealed.
