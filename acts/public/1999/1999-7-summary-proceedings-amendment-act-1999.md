# Summary Proceedings Amendment Act 1999

An Act to amend the Summary Proceedings Act 1957

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title and commencement

**(1) This Act may be cited as the Summary Proceedings Amendment Act 1999, and is part of the Summary Proceedings Act 1957 (**the principal Act**).** **(2) This Act comes into force on the 28th day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.**

## 2 Certain informations to disclose range of penalties

**(1) This subsection inserted s 17A in the principal Act.** **(2) The principal Act applies to proceedings commenced before the commencement of this section, as if subsection (1) had not been enacted.**

## 3 Application of provisions of Part 2

This section inserted s 146(ca) in the principal Act.
