# Ship Registration Amendment Act (No 2) 1999

An Act to amend the Ship Registration Act 1992

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title and commencement

**(1) This Act may be cited as the Ship Registration Amendment Act (No 2) 1999, and is part of the Ship Registration Act 1992 (“the principal Act”).** **(2) This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.**

## 2 New Zealand-owned ships

**(1) This subsection amended s 3(1) of the principal Act.** **(2) Section 3 of the principal Act is amended by repealing subsection (3).**

## 3 Evidence required before registration

This section amended s 14(1)(b) of the principal Act.

## 4 Entry of particulars in Part B of Register

This section inserted s 21(6) of the principal Act.

## 5 Alterations to ships

This section inserted s 30(5) of the principal Act.

## 6 Closure of registration

This section inserted s 32(3A) of the principal Act.

## 7 Discharge, waiver, or extinction of mortgage

This section substituted section 45 of the principal Act.

## 8 Evidence relating to transmissions

This section substituted section 81 of the principal Act.
