# Wild Animal Control Amendment Act 1999

An Act to amend the Wild Animal Control Act 1977

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Wild Animal Control Amendment Act 1999, and is part of the Wild Animal Control Act 1977 (“the principal Act”).

## 2 Interpretation

This section amended Section 2(1) of the principal Act.

## 3 Ownership of wild animals

This section substituted Section 9(2) of the principal Act.

## 4 Deer farms

This section amended Section 12A(8) of the principal Act.

## 5 Safari parks

This section amended Section 12B(4) of the principal Act.
