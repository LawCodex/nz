# Companies Amendment Act 1999

An Act to amend the Companies Act 1993

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Companies Amendment Act 1999, and is part of the Companies Act 1993 (“the principal Act”).

## 2 Liability of shareholders

**(1) This subsection substituted s 98(6)(a) of the principal Act.** **(2) Section 98(6) of the principal Act was amended as follows—** (a) This paragraph amended s 98(6)(b) of the principal Act.

(b) This paragraph amended s 98(6)(c) of the principal Act.

## 3 Commencement of liquidation

This section substituted s 241(5) of the principal Act.

## 4 Commencement of liquidation to be recorded

This section inserted section 241A of the principal Act.

## 5 Interim liquidator

This section inserted s 246(4) to (6) of the principal Act.

## 6 Other duties of liquidator

Section 255(2) of the principal Act is amended— (a) This paragraph amended s 255(2)(a)(ii) of the principal Act.

(b) This paragraph amended s 255(2)(d) of the principal Act.

## 7 Power to disclaim onerous property

This section substituted s 269(2) of the principal Act.

## 8 Transactions having preferential effect

**(1) This subsection substituted s 292(5)(a) of the principal Act.** **(2) Section 292(5) of the principal Act is amended—** (a) This paragraph amended s 292(5)(b) of the principal Act.

(b) This paragraph amended s 292(5)(c) of the principal Act.

**(3) This subsection substituted s 292(6)(a) of the principal Act.** **(4)** (a) This paragraph amended s 292(6)(b) of the principal Act.

(b) This paragraph amended s 292(6)(c) of the principal Act.

**(3) This subsection substituted s 293(7)(a) of the principal Act.** **(4)** (a) This paragraph amended s 293(7)(b) of the principal Act.

(b) This paragraph amended s 293(7)(c) of the principal Act.

## 10 Transactions at undervalue

**(1) This subsection substituted s 297(3)(b)(i) of the principal Act.** **(2) Section 297(3)(b) of the principal Act is amended—** (a) This paragraph amended s 297(3)(b)(ii) of the principal Act.

(b) This paragraph amended 297(3)(b)(iii) of the principal Act.

## 11 Transactions for inadequate or excessive consideration with directors and certain other persons

**(1) This subsection substituted s 298(4)(a) of the principal Act.** **(2) Section 298(4) of the principal Act is amended—** (a) This paragraph amended s 298(4)(b) of the principal Act.
