# Gas Amendment Act 2007

## Note

This Act is administered by the Ministry of Economic Development.

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the Gas Amendment Act 2007.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Principal Act amended

This Act amends the Gas Act 1992.

## 4 Other gas governance regulations

Section 43G(2) is amended by repealing paragraph (c) and substituting the following paragraph: “(c) providing for arrangements to enable consumers to switch gas retailers:”.
