# Trade in Endangered Species Amendment Act 2007

## Note

This Act is administered in the Department of Conservation.

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the Trade in Endangered Species Amendment Act 2007.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Principal Act amended

This Act amends the Trade in Endangered Species Act 1989.

## 4 Seizure of endangered species traded in contravention of Act

**(1) Section 39(1) is amended by omitting “port or aerodrome,” and substituting “port, aerodrome, transitional facility, or Customs controlled area,”.** **(2) Section 39 is amended by adding the following subsection:** “(6) For the purposes of subsection (1),— “**Customs controlled area** has the meaning given to it in section 2(1) of the Customs and Excise Act 1996

“**transitional facility** has the meaning given to it in section 2(1) of the Biosecurity Act 1993.”
