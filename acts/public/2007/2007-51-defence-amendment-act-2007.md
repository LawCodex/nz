# Defence Amendment Act 2007

## Note

This Act is administered by the Ministry of Defence and the New Zealand Defence Force.

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the Defence Amendment Act 2007.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Principal Act amended

This Act amends the Defence Act 1990.

## 4 Amendments to principal Act in Schedule

The principal Act is amended in the manner set out in the Schedule.
