# Coroners Act 2006 Amendment Act 2007

## Note

This Act is administered by the Ministry of Justice.

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the Coroners Act 2006 Amendment Act 2007.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on 1 July 2007.

## 3 Amendments to Coroners Act 2006

This Act amends the Coroners Act 2006.

## 4 Coroners

**(1) Section 103(4) is amended by omitting “68” and substituting “70”.** **(2) Section 103(5) is amended by omitting “68” and substituting “70”.**

## 5 Relief coroners

**(1) Section 104(5) is amended by omitting “68” and substituting “70”.** **(2) Section 104(6) is amended by omitting “68” and substituting “70”.**
