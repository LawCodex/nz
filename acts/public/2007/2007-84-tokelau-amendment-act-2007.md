# Tokelau Amendment Act 2007

## Note

This Act is administered by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Trade.

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the Tokelau Amendment Act 2007.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Principal Act amended

This Act amends the Tokelau Act 1948.

## 4 General Fono may make rules

Section 3A(2)(c) is repealed.

## 5 Restoration of Acts or regulations

Section 3G(1)(a) is repealed.

## 6 Existing laws to continue in force

Section 4A(2) is repealed.

## 7 Amendment to Tokelau Administration Regulations 1993

**(1) This section amends the Tokelau Administration Regulations 1993.** **(2) Regulation 5(2)(ii) is revoked.**

## 8 Amendments to Tokelau Islands Crimes Regulations 1975

**(1) This section amends the Tokelau Islands Crimes Regulations 1975.** **(2) Regulation 2 is amended by omitting the heading and substituting the following heading: “**Sections 243 to 245 of Niue Act 1966 to apply to Tokelau**”.** **(3) Regulation 2(1) is amended by—** (a) omitting “Parts 5, 6, and 7” and substituting “sections 243 to 245” ; and

(b) omitting “the Tokelau Islands” and substituting “Tokelau”.

**(4) Regulation 2(2) is amended by omitting “the Tokelau Islands of the provisions of Parts 5, 6, and 7” and substituting “Tokelau of sections 243 to 245”.** **(5) Regulation 2(2) is amended by revoking paragraph (b) and substituting the following paragraph:** “(b) section 243 of the Niue Act 1966 has effect as if the words ‘of the Cabinet and the Seal of Niue’ were omitted and ‘under the hand of the Administrator of Tokelau’ were substituted.”

**(6) Regulation 3 is revoked.** **(7) The items relating to Niue Government Account, Niuean custom, and Niue Public Service in Schedule 1 are revoked.** **(8) Schedule 2 is revoked.**

## 9 Repeals and revocations

**(1) The enactments set out in Part 1 of the Schedule are repealed.** **(2) The regulations set out in Part 2 of the Schedule are revoked.**
