# Cook Islands Amendment Act 2007

## Note

This Act is administered by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Trade.

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the Cook Islands Amendment Act 2007.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Principal Act amended

This Act amends the Cook Islands Act 1915.

## 4 Interpretation

**(1) Section 2(1) is amended by omitting the definition of **aerodrome** and **aircraft** and substituting the following definition:** “**aircraft** has the same meaning as in the Civil Aviation Act 1964.”

**(2) Section 2(1) is amended by repealing the definitions of **alienation**, **Asiatic**, **bylaw**, **Commission**, **Crown land**, **European land**, **Minister**, **Native custom**, **Native freehold land**, **order**, **Ordinance**, **public place**, **Registrar**, **Rules of Court**, and **will**.**

## 5 Amendments to principal Act

The provisions of the principal Act set out in Schedule 1 are repealed.

## 6 Amendments to other enactments

The provisions of other enactments set out in Schedule 2 are repealed.

## 7 Repeals and revocations

**(1) The enactments set out in Part 1 of Schedule 3 are repealed.** **(2) The regulations set out in Part 2 of Schedule 3 are revoked.**
