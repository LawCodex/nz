# Rates Rebate Amendment Act 2010

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the Rates Rebate Amendment Act 2010.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Principal Act amended

This Act amends the Rates Rebate Act 1973.

## 4 Interpretation

Paragraph (b) of the definition of **income** in section 2(1) is amended by inserting “or partner” after “spouse” in each place where it appears.
