# Coroners Amendment Act 2010

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the Coroners Amendment Act 2010.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Principal Act amended

This Act amends the Coroners Act 2006.

## 4 Regulations

Section 140(a) is amended by inserting “salaries,” before “fees,”.
