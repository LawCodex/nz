# Insolvency Amendment Act 2013

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the Insolvency Amendment Act 2013.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Principal Act

This Act amends the Insolvency Act 2006 (the **principal Act**).

## 4 Section 364 amended (Debtor disqualified from entry to no asset procedure in certain cases)

In section 364(b), replace “the bankrupt” with “he or she”.
