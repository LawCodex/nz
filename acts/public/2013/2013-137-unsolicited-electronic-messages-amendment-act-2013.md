# Unsolicited Electronic Messages Amendment Act 2013

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the Unsolicited Electronic Messages Amendment Act 2013.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Principal Act

This Act amends the Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007 (the **principal Act**).

## 4 Section 58 amended (Regulations)

Repeal section 58(k).
