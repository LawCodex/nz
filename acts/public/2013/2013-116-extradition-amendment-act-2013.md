# Extradition Amendment Act 2013

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the Extradition Amendment Act 2013.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Principal Act

This Act amends the Extradition Act 1999 (the **principal Act**).

## 4 Section 101B amended (Certain crimes with transnational aspects deemed to be included in extradition treaties)

In section 101B(1)(a), replace “257A” with “243”.
