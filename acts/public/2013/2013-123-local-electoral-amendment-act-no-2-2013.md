# Local Electoral Amendment Act (No 2) 2013

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the Local Electoral Amendment Act (No 2) 2013.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Principal Act

This Act amends the Local Electoral Act 2001 (the **principal Act**).

## 4 Section 138 amended (Duty to take action in respect of offences)

Replace section 138(1)(a)(ii) with:

“(ii) Part 5A; or

“(iii) this Part; or”.
