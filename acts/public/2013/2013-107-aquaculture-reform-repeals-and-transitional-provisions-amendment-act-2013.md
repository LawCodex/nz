# Aquaculture Reform (Repeals and Transitional Provisions) Amendment Act 2013

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the Aquaculture Reform (Repeals and Transitional Provisions) Amendment Act 2013.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Principal Act

This Act amends the Aquaculture Reform (Repeals and Transitional Provisions) Act 2004 (the **principal Act**).

## 4 Section 20A amended (Marine Farming Permit 364 for Waikato Communal Area)

In section 20A(8), replace “325° 805 metres from Trig 238” with “352° 805 metres from Trig 23B”.
