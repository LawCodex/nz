# Oaths and Declarations Amendment Act 1998

An Act to amend the Oaths and Declarations Act 1957

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title and commencement

**(1) This Act may be cited as the Oaths and Declarations Amendment Act 1998, and is part of the Oaths and Declarations Act 1957 (“the principal Act”).** **(2) This Act comes into force on the date on which this Act receives the Royal assent.**

## 2 Unlawful administration of oaths

This section amended s 6(3) of the principal Act.

## 3 Persons required to take judicial oath

This section amended Schedule 2 of the principal Act.
