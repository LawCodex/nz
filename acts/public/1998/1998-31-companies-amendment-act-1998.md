# Companies Amendment Act 1998

An Act to amend the Companies Act 1993

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Companies Amendment Act 1998, and is part of the Companies Act 1993 (“the principal Act”).

## 2 Meaning of **redeemable**

This section substituted s 68 of the principal Act.

## 3 Liability of former shareholders

This section amended s 98(6)(b) and inserted s 98(6)(c) of the principal Act.

## 4 Annual meeting of shareholders

This section repealed s 120(1)(a) of the principal Act.

## 5 Indemnity and insurance

This section substituted s 162(5)(c) of the principal Act.

## 6 Contents of annual report

**(1)** (a) This paragraph repealed s 211(1)(d) of the principal Act.

(b) This paragraph amended s 211(1)(f) of the principal Act.

(c) This paragraph amended s 211(1)(h) of the principal Act.

**(2) This subsection amended s 211(2) of the principal Act.** **(3) This subsection substituted s 211(3) and inserted s 211(4) of the principal Act.**

## 7 Registrar may alter New Zealand register

This section inserted s 214A of the principal Act.

## 8 Short form amalgamation

This section amended s 222(2) of the principal Act.

## 9 Liquidator to summon meeting of creditors

**(1)** (a) This paragraph amended s 243(1)(a) of the principal Act.

(b) This paragraph amended s 243(1)(b) of the principal Act.

**(2) This subsection inserted s 243(1A) of the principal Act.** **(3) This subsection amended s 243(2)(b) of the principal Act.** **(4) This subsection amended s 243(4) of the principal Act.** **(5) This subsection inserted s 243(4A) of the principal Act.**

## 10 Other duties of liquidator

This section inserted s 255(6) of the principal Act.

## 11 Duties in relation to accounts

This section amended s 256(2)(b) of the principal Act.

## 12 Transactions having preferential effect

**(1) This subsection amended s 292(5)(b) and inserted s 292(5)(c) of the principal Act.** **(2) This subsection amended s 292(6)(b) and inserted s 292(6)(c) of the principal Act.**

## 13 Voidable charges

**(1) This section amended s 293(6)(b) and inserted s 293(6)(c) of the principal Act.** **(2) This section amended s 293(7)(b) and inserted s 293(7)(c) of the principal Act.**

## 14 Transactions at undervalue

This section amended s 297(3)(b)(ii) and inserted s 297(3)(b)(iii) of the principal Act.

## 15 Transactions for inadequate or excessive consideration with directors and certain other persons

This section amended s 298(4)(b) and inserted s 298(4)(c) of the principal Act.

## 16 Mutual credit and set-off

**(1) This subsection amended s 310(6)(b) and inserted s 310(6)(c) of the principal Act.** **(2) This subsection amended s 310(7)(b) and inserted s 310(7)(c) of the principal Act.**

## 17 Establishment of Liquidation Surplus Account

This section amended s 316(1) of the principal Act.

## 18 Grounds for removal from register

This section amended s 318(1)(e) of the principal Act.

## 19 Service of documents on companies in legal proceedings

This section amended s 387(1)(e) and inserted s 387(1)(f) of the principal Act.

## 20 Schedule 1 amended

**(1) This section amended cl 5(4)(c) and inserted cl 5(4)(d) of Schedule 1 of the principal Act.** **(2) This section amended cl 6(3) of Schedule 1 of the principal Act.**

## 21 Schedule 5 amended

**(1) This subsection amended cl 2(2)(c) and inserted cl 2(2)(d) of Schedule 5 of the principal Act.** **(2) This subsection substituted cl 3(2) and inserted cl 3(2A) of Schedule 5 of the principal Act.** **(3) This subsection inserted cl 7(1A) of Schedule 5 of the principal Act.** **(4) This subsection inserted cl 11 of Schedule 5 of the principal Act.**
