# Broadcasting Amendment Act 1998

An Act to amend the Broadcasting Act 1989

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Broadcasting Amendment Act 1998, and is part of the Broadcasting Act 1989 (“the principal Act”).

## 2 Offences

This section amended s 14(b) of the principal Act.

## 3 Schedule 1 amended

This section amended clause 16 of Schedule 1 of the principal Act.
