# Ngarimu VC and 28th (Maori) Battalion Memorial Scholarship Fund Amendment Act 1998

An Act to amend the Ngarimu VC and 28th (Maori) Battalion Memorial Scholarship Fund Act 1945

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title and commencement

**(1) This Act may be cited as the Ngarimu VC and 28th (Maori) Battalion Memorial Scholarship Fund Amendment Act 1998, and is part of the Ngarimu VC and 28th (Maori) Battalion Memorial Scholarship Fund Act 1945 (“The principal Act”).** **(2) This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.**

## 2 Establishment of Board to administer Fund

**(1) This subsection inserted s 4(2)(aa) in the principal Act.** **(2) This subsection substituted ss 4(2)(c) and 4(2)(d) of the principal Act.**
