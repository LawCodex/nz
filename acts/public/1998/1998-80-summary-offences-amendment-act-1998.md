# Summary Offences Amendment Act 1998

An Act to amend the Summary Offences Act 1981

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title and commencement

**(1) This Act may be cited as the Summary Offences Amendment Act 1998, and is part of the Summary Offences Act 1981 (“the principal Act”).** **(2) This Act comes into force on the date on which this Act receives the Royal assent.**

## 2 Imitation of Court documents

This section amended s 18(1)(a) and (3)(b)(i) of the principal Act.

## 3 Jurisdiction

This section amended s 40(2) of the principal Act.
