# Maritime Transport Amendment Act (No 2) 1998

An Act to amend the Maritime Transport Act 1994

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title and commencement

**(1) This Act may be cited as the Maritime Transport Amendment Act (No 2) 1998, and is part of the Maritime Transport Act 1994 (“the principal Act”).** **(2) This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.**

## 2 New headings and sections inserted

*This section inserted ss 200A–200C of the principal Act*.

## 3 Regulations

*This section amended s 201(1)(d) of the principal Act*.

## 4 Proceedings for offences

Section 419(2) of the principal Act is repealed.

## 5 Infringement offences

*This section amended s 422(1) of the principal Act*.

## 6 Infringement notices

*This section inserted s 423(1A) in the principal Act*. This Act is administered by the Ministry of Transport.
