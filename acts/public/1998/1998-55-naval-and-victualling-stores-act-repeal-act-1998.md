# Naval and Victualling Stores Act Repeal Act 1998

An Act to repeal the Naval and Victualling Stores Act 1908

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Naval and Victualling Stores Act Repeal Act 1998.

## 2 Naval and Victualling Stores Act 1908 repealed

**(1) The Naval and Victualling Stores Act 1908 is repealed.** **(2) Schedule 1 of the Defence Act 1990 is consequentially amended by repealing the item relating to section 10 of the Naval and Victualling Stores Act 1908.** This Act is administered by the New Zealand Defence Force.
