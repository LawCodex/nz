# Bylaws Amendment Act 1998

An Act to amend the Bylaws Act 1910

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Bylaws Amendment Act 1998, and is part of the Bylaws Act 1910 (“the principal Act”).

## 2 Bylaw may provide fine for breach thereof

This section amended s 20 of the principal Act.

## 3 Repeal of provisions relating to confirmation of bylaws

This section repealed ss 3 to 11 of the principal Act.

## 4 Continuation of certificates of confirmation

Every certificate of confirmation issued under the principal Act before the coming into force of this Act remains in force, and the provisions of sections 7 to 10 of the principal Act continue to apply to such a certificate as if those provisions had not been repealed.
