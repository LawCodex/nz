# Agricultural Compounds and Veterinary Medicines Amendment Act 1998

## Note

This Act is administered in the new Ministry.

An Act to amend the Agricultural Compounds and Veterinary Medicines Act 1997

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Agricultural Compounds and Veterinary Medicines Amendment Act 1998, and is part of the Agricultural Compounds and Veterinary Medicines Act 1997 (“the principal Act”).

## 2 Amendment to Schedule 2

This section amended schedule 2 of the principal Act.
