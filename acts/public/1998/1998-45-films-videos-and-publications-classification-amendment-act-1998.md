# Films, Videos, and Publications Classification Amendment Act 1998

An Act to amend the Films, Videos, and Publications Classification Act 1993

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Films, Videos, and Publications Classification Amendment Act 1998, and is part of the Films, Videos, and Publications Classification Act 1993 (“the principal Act”).

## 2 Community representatives

This section substituted s 74(2) of the principal Act.
