# Disputes Tribunals Amendment Act 1998

An Act to amend The Disputes Tribunals Act 1988

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title and commencement

**(1) This Act may be cited as the Disputes Tribunals Amendment Act 1998, and is part of The Disputes Tribunals Act 1988 (“the principal Act”).** **(2) This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.**

## 2 Interpretation

This section inserted the definition of the term **Principal Disputes Referee** in s 2 of the principal Act.

## 3 New sections substituted

This section substituted s 6, and inserted ss 6A to 6C of the principal Act.

## 4 Appointment of Referees

This section amended s 7(4) of the principal Act.

## 5 Salary and allowances of Principal Disputes Referee

This section inserted s 8A of the principal Act.

## 6 Jurisdiction of Tribunals

This section amended s 10(3) of the principal Act.

## 7 Extension of jurisdiction by agreement between the parties

This section amended s 13(2) of the principal Act.

## 8 Orders of Tribunal

(a) This paragraph amended s 19(5), (6), and (7) of the principal Act.

(b) This paragraph amended s 19(7) of the principal Act.

## 9 Rules

This section amended s 60(2)(a) of the principal Act.

The enactments specified in the Schedule are amended in the manner indicated in that schedule.
