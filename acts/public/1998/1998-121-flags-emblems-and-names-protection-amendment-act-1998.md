# Flags, Emblems, and Names Protection Amendment Act 1998

An Act to amend the Flags, Emblems, and Names Protection Act 1981

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title and commencement

**(1) This Act may be cited as the Flags, Emblems, and Names Protection Amendment Act 1998, and is part of the Flags, Emblems, and Names Protection Act 1981 (“the principal Act”).** **(2) This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.**

## 2 New sections inserted

This section inserted ss 20A and 20B in the principal Act.

## 3 General penalty

This section substituted s 24 of the principal Act.

## 4 Power to amend Schedule 2A

This section inserted s 26A in the principal Act.

## 5 New Schedule inserted

The principal Act is amended by inserting, after Schedule 2, the Schedule 2A set out in the Schedule of this Act.

## 6 Expiry of Part 3 of Schedule 2A

Part 3 of Schedule 2A expires at the close of 30 June 2001.
