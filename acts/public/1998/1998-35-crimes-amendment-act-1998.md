# Crimes Amendment Act 1998

An Act to amend the Crimes Act 1961

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Crimes Amendment Act 1998, and is part of the Crimes Act 1961 (“the principal Act”).

## 2 Money laundering

**(1) This subsection amended s 257A(2) and (3) of the principal Act.** **(2) This subsection substituted s 257A(6) and inserted s 257A(6A) and (6B) of the principal Act.**

## 3 When bail not allowable

\[Repealed\]

Section 3 was repealed, as from 1 January 2001, by section 74(2) Bail Act 2000 (2000 No 38). *See* section 75 of that Act as to the savings provisions.

## 4 Refusal of witness to give evidence

This section substituted s 352(3) of the principal Act.

## 5 Appeal against order in respect of costs

This section inserted s 379CA of the principal Act.
