# Summary Proceedings Amendment Act (No 3) 1998

An Act to amend the Summary Proceedings Act 1957

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title and commencement

**(1) This Act may be cited as the Summary Proceedings Amendment Act (No 3) 1998, and is part of the Summary Proceedings Act 1957 (“the principal Act”).** **(2) This Act comes into force on 1 November 1998.**

## 2 Interpretation

This section inserted the definitions of the terms **Bank** and **Benefit** in s 2(1) of the principal Act.

## 3 Summary procedure for infringement offences

**(1) This subsection inserted s 21(3A) to (3D) of the principal Act.** **(2) This subsection inserted s 21(5A) and (5B) of the principal Act.** **(3) This subsection inserted s 21(13) of the principal Act.**

## 4 Withdrawal of warrant

This section inserted s 23(2) of the principal Act.

## 5 Withdrawal of information by informant

This section inserted s 36(1B) of the principal Act.

## 6 Registrar may receive not guilty pleas

This section inserted s 41A of the principal Act.

## 7 Registrar may prohibit publication of names

This section inserted s 46A of the principal Act.

## 8 Variation of conditions of bail

This section substituted s 50A(2)(b) of the principal Act.

## 9 Powers of Registrar to adjourn hearing or issue warrant to arrest defendant

This section substituted s 61A(3) and inserted s 61A(4) of the principal Act.

## 10 Defendant's right to elect trial by jury where offence punishable by more than 3 months' imprisonment

**(1) This section inserted s 66(1A) of the principal Act.** **(2) This subsection amended s 66(2) of the principal Act.**

## 11 Registrar may receive elections

This section inserted s 66A of the principal Act.

## 12 Conduct of hearing

This section substituted s 67(1A) of the principal Ac.

## 13 District Court Judge or Justice or Registrar may grant a rehearing

**(1) This section inserted s 75(1AA) of the principal Act.** **(2) This section amended s 75(3) of the principal Act.**

This section inserted paragraphs (c) to (f) of the definition of the term **salary or wages** in s 79 of the principal Act.
