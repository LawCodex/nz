# Forestry Rights Registration Amendment Act 1998

An Act to amend the Forestry Rights Registration Act 1983

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Forestry Rights Registration Amendment Act 1998, and is part of the Forestry Rights Registration Act 1983 (**the principal Act**).

## 2 Definitions

**(1) This subsection omitted the definitions of the terms **forestry right**, **grantor**, and **grantee** from s 2 of the principal.** **(2) This subsection inserted the definitions of the terms **Forestry right**, **Holder** and **Proprietor** in s 2 of the principal Act.**

## 3 Creation of forestry rights

This section inserted s 2A of the principal Act.

(b) This paragraph amended s 3(2) of the principal Act.
