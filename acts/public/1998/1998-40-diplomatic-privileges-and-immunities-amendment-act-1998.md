# Diplomatic Privileges and Immunities Amendment Act 1998

An Act to amend the Diplomatic Privileges and Immunities Act 1968

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Diplomatic Privileges and Immunities Amendment Act 1998, and is part of the Diplomatic Privileges and Immunities Act 1968 (“the principal Act”).

## 2 Schedule 2 amended

This section substituted cl 4 of Schedule 2 of the principal Act.
