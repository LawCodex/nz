# Dog Control Amendment Act 2012

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the Dog Control Amendment Act 2012.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Principal Act amended

This Act amends the Dog Control Act 1996.

## 4 Interpretation

Paragraph (b) of the definition of **stock** in section 2 is amended by omitting “thar” and substituting “tahr”.
