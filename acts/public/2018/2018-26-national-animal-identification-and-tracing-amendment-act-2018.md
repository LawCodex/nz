# National Animal Identification and Tracing Amendment Act 2018

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the National Animal Identification and Tracing Amendment Act 2018.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Principal Act

This Act amends the National Animal Identification and Tracing Act 2012 (the **principal Act**).

## Part 1 Amendments to principal Act

### 4 Section 4 amended (Interpretation)

**(1)** In section 4, replace the definition of **animal movement** with:

**animal movement** means the movement of 1 or more NAIT animals between 2 locations, either of which is, or both of which are, a NAIT location or other location

**(2)** In section 4, insert in its appropriate alphabetical order:

**other location** means a place, other than a NAIT location, where 1 or more NAIT animals are kept or held

### 5 Section 24 amended (Key obligations)

In section 24(2)(d), after “location”, insert “or other location”.

### 6 Section 30 amended (Obligation to identify and register NAIT animals)

In section 30(2), after “location”, insert “or other location”.

### 7 Section 31 amended (Obligation to declare movement of NAIT animals)

Replace section 31(1) with:

**(1)** Every PICA must declare every animal movement to the NAIT organisation.

### 8 Schedule 2 amended

**(1)** In Schedule 2, clause 1(1), replace the definition of **search power** with:

**search power**, in relation to any provision in this schedule, means—

(a) every search warrant issued under this Act; and

(b) every power conferred by or under this Act to enter and inspect (without warrant) any place, vehicle, or other thing

**(2)** In Schedule 2, clause 35(1), after “form”, insert “, if any”.

**(3)** In Schedule 2, clause 82(2), after “for a NAIT location”, insert “or other location”.

**(4)** In Schedule 2, replace clause 82(2)(b) with:

(b) to comply with section 31 in relation to animal movements:

### 9 Review

**(1)** The Minister must, 12 months after the commencement of this Act, initiate a review of the amendments set out in section 8.

**(2)** The Minister must present a report on the review to the House of Representatives within 3 months of the initiation of the review under subsection (1).

## Part 2 Amendments to regulations

### Amendments to National Animal Identification and Tracing (Infringement Offences) Regulations 2012

### 10 Amendments to National Animal Identification and Tracing (Infringement Offences) Regulations 2012

**(1)** This section amends the National Animal Identification and Tracing (Infringement Offences) Regulations 2012.

**(2)** In Schedule 1, item relating to clause 82(2)(a) of Schedule 2 of the Act, after “NAIT location”, insert “or other location”.

**(3)** In Schedule 1, item relating to clause 82(2)(b) of Schedule 2 of the Act,—

(a) after “for a NAIT location”, insert “or other location”; and

(b) replace “a NAIT animal consigned for transport or droving from a NAIT location registered by the PICA” with “animal movements”.

**(4)** In Schedule 1, item relating to regulations 5(5B) and 27(1) of the National Animal Identification and Tracing (Obligations and Exemptions) Regulations 2012, replace “transport or droving between 2 NAIT locations” with “any animal movement”.

### Amendment to National Animal Identification and Tracing (Obligations and Exemptions) Regulations 2012

### 11 Amendment to National Animal Identification and Tracing (Obligations and Exemptions) Regulations 2012

**(1)** This section amends the National Animal Identification and Tracing (Obligations and Exemptions) Regulations 2012.

**(2)** In regulation 5(5B), replace “transport or droving between 2 NAIT locations” with “any animal movement”.
