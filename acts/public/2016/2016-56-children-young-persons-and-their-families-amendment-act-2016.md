# Children, Young Persons, and Their Families Amendment Act 2016

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the Children, Young Persons, and Their Families Amendment Act 2016.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on 1 March 2017.

## 3 Principal Act

This Act amends the Children, Young Persons, and Their Families Act 1989 (the **principal Act**).

## 4 Section 434 amended (Principal Youth Court Judge)

After section 434(7), insert:

**(8)** The Chief District Court Judge may authorise a Youth Court Judge to act in place of the Principal Youth Court Judge if—

(a) the Principal Youth Court Judge is absent for any reason; or

(b) the office of the Principal Youth Court Judge is vacant.
