# Insolvency Amendment Act 2016

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the Insolvency Amendment Act 2016.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on 1 March 2017.

## 3 Principal Act

This Act amends the Insolvency Act 2006 (the **principal Act**).

## 4 Section 6 amended (Corporations and other entities not subject to Act)

In section 6, insert as subsection (2):

**(2)** However,—

(a) Part 16 of the Companies Act 1993 applies to the liquidation of companies, overseas companies, and associations within the meaning of section 2(1) of that Act; and

(b) other bodies corporate may be put into liquidation under or in accordance with the Acts under which they are incorporated or registered.
