# Official Information (Parliamentary Under-Secretaries) Amendment Act 2016

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the Official Information (Parliamentary Under-Secretaries) Amendment Act 2016.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Principal Act

This Act amends the Official Information Act 1982 (the **principal Act**).

## 4 Section 2 amended (Interpretation)

In section 2(1), insert in its appropriate alphabetical order:

**Minister of the Crown** includes a Parliamentary Under-Secretary
