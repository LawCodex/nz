# Health and Disability Services (Safety) Amendment Act 2005

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

**(1) This Act is the Health and Disability Services (Safety) Amendment Act 2005.** **(2) In this Act, the Health and Disability Services (Safety) Act 2001 is called “the principal Act”.**

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Health or disability services defined

Section 5(1)(c) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the word “Services”.
