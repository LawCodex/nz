# National Parks Amendment Act 2005

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

**(1) This Act is the National Parks Amendment Act 2005.** **(2) In this Act, the National Parks Act 1980 is called “the principal Act”.**

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 General policy for parks

Section 44(6) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the words “, without limiting section 19 of this Act,”.
