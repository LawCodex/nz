# Child Support Amendment Act 2005

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

**(1) This Act is the Child Support Amendment Act 2005.** **(2) In this Act, the Child Support Act 1991 is called “the principal Act”.**

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on 26 April 2005.

## 3 Amendments to principal Act in Schedule

The principal Act is amended in the manner set out in the Schedule.

| Schedule<br>Amendments to principal Act | s 3 |
|---|---|

## Section 2

In the definition of **assessment**, replace “spousal maintenance” with “domestic maintenance”.

After the definition of **Commissioner**, insert the following:

“**domestic maintenance** means any payment required to be made under this Act by any person towards the support of another person under section 58(2) or section 68(2) of this Act”.

In the definition of **financial support**, replace “spousal maintenance”, in both places that it occurs, with “domestic maintenance”.

Replace the definition of **liable spouse** with the following:

“**liable spouse or partner** means a person who is liable to pay domestic maintenance under section 58(2) or section 68(2) of this Act.”

Omit the definition of **married person**.
