# Copyright Amendment Act 2005

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

**(1) This Act is the Copyright Amendment Act 2005.** **(2) In this Act, the Copyright Act 1994 is called “the principal Act”.**

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 No copyright in certain works

Section 27 of the principal Act is amended by inserting, after subsection (1), the following subsections: “(1A) No Crown copyright exists in any work, whenever that work was made,— “(a) in which the Crown copyright has not been assigned to another person; and

“(b) that is incorporated by reference in a work referred to in subsection (1).

“(1B) Except as specified in subsection (1A), nothing in subsection (1) affects copyright in any work that is incorporated by reference in a work referred to in subsection (1).”
