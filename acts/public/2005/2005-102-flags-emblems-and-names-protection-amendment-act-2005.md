# Flags, Emblems, and Names Protection Amendment Act 2005

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

**(1) This Act is the Flags, Emblems, and Names Protection Amendment Act 2005.** **(2) In this Act, the Flags, Emblems, and Names Protection Act 1981 is called “the principal Act”.**

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Unauthorised use of name Returned Services' Association

Section 18(3) of the principal Act is amended by— (a) omitting from paragraph (a) the expression “:”, and substituting the word “; or”:

(b) omitting from paragraph (b)(i) the words “New Zealand Returned Services' Association”, and substituting the words “Royal New Zealand Returned and Services' Association”:

(c) omitting from paragraph (b)(ii) the words “of which at least half the members are”, and substituting the words “whose members include”:

(d) omitting from paragraph (b)(iv) the words “of which at least half the members are”, and substituting the words “whose members include”:

(e) omitting from paragraph (b)(iv) the words “New Zealand Returned Services' Association”, and substituting the words “Royal New Zealand Returned and Services' Association”:

(f) omitting from paragraph (b)(iv) the expression “:”, and substituting the word “; or”.
