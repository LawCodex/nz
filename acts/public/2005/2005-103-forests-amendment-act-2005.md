# Forests Amendment Act 2005

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

**(1) This Act is the Forests Amendment Act 2005.** **(2) In this Act, the Forests Act 1949 is called “the principal Act”.**

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Delegation of Minister's powers

Section 4 of the principal Act is repealed.

## 4 Delegation of powers by Secretary

Section 4A of the principal Act is repealed.
