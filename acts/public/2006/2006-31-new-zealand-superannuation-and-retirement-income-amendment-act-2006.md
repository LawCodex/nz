# New Zealand Superannuation and Retirement Income Amendment Act 2006

## Note

This Act is administered by the Ministry of Social Development and the Treasury.

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the New Zealand Superannuation and Retirement Income Amendment Act 2006.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on 1 July 2006.

## 3 Principal Act amended

This Act amends the New Zealand Superannuation and Retirement Income Act 2001.

## 4 Living alone payments

Section 13(3)(b) is amended by omitting “or household expenses (including accommodation expenses)”.

## 5 Entitlement to benefits for spouses or partners of persons receiving means-tested residential disability services

**(1) The heading to section 17 is amended by omitting “**receiving means-tested residential disability services**” and substituting “**in long-term residential care in hospital or rest home**”.** **(2) Section 17 is amended by repealing subsection (1) and substituting the following subsection:** “(1) This section applies to any person who is not in long-term residential care and whose spouse or partner is in long-term residential care in a hospital or rest home, whether or not the spouse or partner is a resident assessed as requiring care (within the meaning of section 136 of the Social Security Act 1964).”

## 6 Living alone rate of payment for spouses or partners of persons receiving means-tested residential disability services

The heading to section 18 is amended by omitting “**receiving means-tested residential disability services**” and substituting “**in long-term residential care in hospital or rest home**”.

## 7 Absences up to 52 weeks with aid agencies

**(1) The heading to section 24 is amended by omitting “**52**” and substituting “**156**”.** **(2) Section 24(1)(b) is amended by omitting “52” and substituting “156”.**
