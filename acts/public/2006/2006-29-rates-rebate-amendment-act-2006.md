# Rates Rebate Amendment Act 2006

## Note

This Act is administered by the Department of Internal Affairs.

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the Rates Rebate Amendment Act 2006.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on 1 July 2006.

## 3 Principal Act amended

This Act amends the Rates Rebate Act 1973.

## 4 Interpretation

**(1) The definition of **rates** in section 2(1) is amended by—** (a) inserting “or uniform annual general charge” after “targeted rate” ; and

(b) repealing paragraph (a).

**(2) The definition of **residential property** in section 2(1) is amended by omitting “rateable” and substituting “rating”.**
