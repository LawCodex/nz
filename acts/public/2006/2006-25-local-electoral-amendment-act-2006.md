# Local Electoral Amendment Act 2006

## Note

This Act is administered by the Department of Internal Affairs.

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the Local Electoral Amendment Act 2006.

## 1 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Principal Act amended

This Act amends the Local Electoral Act 2001.

## 4 Territorial authority or regional council may resolve to establish Maori wards or Maori constituencies

Section 19Z(4) is amended by omitting “clauses 2(4)” and substituting “clauses 2(5)”.

## 5 Schedule 1A amended

**(1) Clause 2(5) of Schedule 1A is amended by omitting “other”.** **(2) Clause 4(4) of Schedule 1A is amended by omitting “other”.**
