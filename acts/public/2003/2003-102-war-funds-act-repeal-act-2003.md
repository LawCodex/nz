# War Funds Act Repeal Act 2003

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the War Funds Act Repeal Act 2003.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 War Funds Act 1915 repealed

The War Funds Act 1915 (1915 No 41) is repealed.
