# New Zealand Railways Corporation Amendment Act 2003

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

**(1) This Act is the New Zealand Railways Corporation Amendment Act 2003.** **(2) In this Act, the New Zealand Railways Corporation Act 1981 is called “the principal Act”.**

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Unused lands and buildings may be leased

**(1) The heading to section 33 of the principal Act is amended by omitting the words “Unused lands”, and substituting the word “Lands”.** **(2) Section 33(3) of the principal Act is amended by repealing paragraph (b) of the proviso, and substituting the following paragraphs:** “(b) where the rent payable under the lease is not less than an annual rent fixed by a valuation made by a registered valuer; or

“(c) where paragraph (a) does not apply and the Corporation considers that it would be impracticable or unreasonable to obtain a valuation for the purposes of paragraph (b).”

**(3) Section 33 of the principal Act is amended by adding the following subsection:** “(7) In subsection (3), registered valuer means a valuer registered under the Valuers Act 1948.”
