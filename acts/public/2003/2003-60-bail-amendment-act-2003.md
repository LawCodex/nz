# Bail Amendment Act 2003

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

**(1) This Act is the Bail Amendment Act 2003.** **(2) In this Act, the Bail Act 2000 is called “the principal Act”.**

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Variation of conditions of bail

Section 57(3) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the words “in writing”.

## 4 Arrest of defendant charged with drug dealing offence

Section 59(2) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the expression “48”, and substituting the expression “72”.
