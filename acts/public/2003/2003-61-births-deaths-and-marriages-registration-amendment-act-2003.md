# Births, Deaths, and Marriages Registration Amendment Act 2003

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

**(1) This Act is the Births, Deaths, and Marriages Registration Amendment Act 2003.** **(2) In this Act, the Births, Deaths, and Marriages Registration Act 1995 is called “the principal Act”.**

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Schedule 1A amended

Schedule 1A of the principal Act is amended by inserting, in the appropriate alphabetical order, the following item:

| Accident Compensation Corporation | Birth information | To verify the identity of a claimant, and a claimant's eligibility or continuing eligibility for a benefit |
|---|---|---|
|  | Marriage information | To verify a claimant's change of name |
|  | Death information | To identify deceased claimants |
