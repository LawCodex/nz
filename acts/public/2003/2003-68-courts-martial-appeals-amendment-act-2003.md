# Courts Martial Appeals Amendment Act 2003

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

**(1) This Act is the Courts Martial Appeals Amendment Act 2003.** **(2) In this Act, the Courts Martial Appeals Act 1953 is called “the principal Act”.**

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Title amended

The Title of the principal Act is amended by omitting the words “**and certain naval disciplinary courts**”.
