# Maori Land Amendment and Maori Land Claims Adjustment Amendment Act 2003

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

**(1) This Act is the Maori Land Amendment and Maori Land Claims Adjustment Amendment Act 2003.** **(2) In this Act, the Maori Land Amendment and Maori Land Claims Adjustment Act 1926 is called “the principal Act”.**

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Bed of Lake Taupo vested in Crown

The proviso to section 14(9)(c) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the expression “50”, and substituting the expression “200”.
