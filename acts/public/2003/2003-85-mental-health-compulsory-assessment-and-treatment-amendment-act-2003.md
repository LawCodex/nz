# Mental Health (Compulsory Assessment and Treatment) Amendment Act 2003

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

**(1) This Act is the Mental Health (Compulsory Assessment and Treatment) Amendment Act 2003.** **(2) In this Act, the Mental Health (Compulsory Assessment and Treatment) Act 1992 is called “the principal Act”.**

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Use of force

Section 122B(3) of the principal Act is amended by inserting, after the words “to whom”, the words “section 58 or”.
