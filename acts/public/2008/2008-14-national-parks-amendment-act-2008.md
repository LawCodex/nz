# National Parks Amendment Act 2008

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the National Parks Amendment Act 2008.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Principal Act amended

This Act amends the National Parks Act 1980.

## 4 Approval of management plans

Section 48(1) is amended by omitting “47(4)” and substituting “47(6)”.
