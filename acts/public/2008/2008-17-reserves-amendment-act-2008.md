# Reserves Amendment Act 2008

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the Reserves Amendment Act 2008.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Principal Act amended

This Act amends the Reserves Act 1977.

## 4 Powers of Commissioner of Crown Lands

The heading to section 62 is amended by omitting “**of Crown Lands**”.
