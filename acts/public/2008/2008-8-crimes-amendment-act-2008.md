# Crimes Amendment Act 2008

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the Crimes Amendment Act 2008.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Principal Act amended

This Act amends the Crimes Act 1961.

## 4 Arrest of persons found committing certain crimes

Section 35(a) is amended by omitting “that is punishable by death or”.
