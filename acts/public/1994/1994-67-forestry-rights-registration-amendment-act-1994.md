# Forestry Rights Registration Amendment Act 1994

## Note

This Act is administered in the Ministry of Forestry.

An Act to amend the Forestry Rights Registration Act 1983

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Forestry Rights Registration Amendment Act 1994, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Forestry Rights Registration Act 1983 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
