# Industrial and Provident Societies Amendment Act 1994

## Note

This Act is administered in the Department of Justice.

An Act to amend the Industrial and Provident Societies Act 1908

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Industrial and Provident Societies Amendment Act 1994, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Industrial and Provident Societies Act 1908 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
