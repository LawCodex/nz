# New Zealand Film Commission Amendment Act 1994

## Note

This Act is administered in the Ministry of Cultural Affairs.

An Act to amend the New Zealand Film Commission Act 1978

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title

This Act may be cited as the New Zealand Film Commission Amendment Act 1994, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the New Zealand Film Commission Act 1978 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
