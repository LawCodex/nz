# Conservation Amendment Act 1994

## Note

This Act is administered in the Department of Conservation.

An Act to amend the Conservation Act 1987

## 1 Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Conservation Amendment Act 1994, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the Conservation Act 1987 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).

## 11 New sections inserted

**(1)** **(2) Every person who, at the close of the 60-day period beginning on the commencement of this section, holds a warrant that—** (a) Appoints the person both as an honorary warranted officer under section 59(2) of the principal Act and as an honorary ranger under section 38(2) of the Wildlife Act 1953; and

(b) Expires on the 30th day of September 1995—

shall, at the close of that 60-day period, cease to hold such appointments and be deemed to be appointed as a fish and game ranger under section 26FA(2) of the principal Act.
