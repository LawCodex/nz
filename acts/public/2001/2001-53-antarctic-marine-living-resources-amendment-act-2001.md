# Antarctic Marine Living Resources Amendment Act 2001

## Note

This Act is administered in the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Trade.

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

**(1) This Act is the Antarctic Marine Living Resources Amendment Act 2001.** **(2) In this Act, the Antarctic Marine Living Resources Act 1981 is called “the principal Act”.**

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Offence to take marine organism without permit

Section 7(1) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the expression “$10,000”, and substituting the expression “$250,000”.

## 4 Offences and penalties

Section 13 of the principal Act is amended— (a) by omitting from subsection (3)(a) the expression “$5,000”, and substituting the expression “$100,000”:

(b) by omitting from subsection (3)(b) the expression “$1,000”, and substituting the expression “$100,000”.
