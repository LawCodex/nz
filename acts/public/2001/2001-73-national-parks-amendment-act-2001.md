# National Parks Amendment Act 2001

## Note

This Act is administered in the Department of Conservation.

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

**(1) This Act is the National Parks Amendment Act 2001.** **(2) In this Act, the National Parks Act 1980 is called “the principal Act”.**

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Existing national parks

Section 6 of the principal Act is amended by adding the following subsection: “(4) By virtue of Order in Council dated 3 April 2000 (*Gazette* 1 June 2000, p 1281), Urewera National Park is now called Te Urewera National Park.”
