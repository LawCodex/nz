# Dog Control (Hearing Dogs) Amendment Act 2001

## Note

This Act is administered in the Department of Internal Affairs.

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

**(1) This Act is the Dog Control (Hearing Dogs) Amendment Act 2001.** **(2) In this Act, the Dog Control Act 1996 is called the “principal Act”.**

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Interpretation

Section 2 of the principal Act is amended by omitting from the definition of **hearing ear dog** the words “the Hearing Association of New Zealand (Incorporated)”, and substituting the words “Hearing Dogs for Deaf People New Zealand Trust”.

## 4 Guide dogs and companion dogs

**(1) Section 75 of the principal Act is amended by inserting in the section heading, after the words “guide dogs”, the words “, hearing ear dogs”.** **(2) Section 75 of the principal Act is amended by inserting, after the words “guide dog” in both places where they occur, the words “, hearing ear dog”.** **(3) Section 75 of the principal Act is amended by inserting, after the words “partially sighted person”, the words “, a deaf or hearing impaired person,”**
