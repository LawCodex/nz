# Pork Industry Board Amendment Act 2001

## Note

This Act is administered in the Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry.

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

**(1) This Act is the Pork Industry Board Amendment Act 2001.** **(2) In this Act, the Pork Industry Board Act 1997 is called “the principal Act”.**

## 2 Commencement

**(1) Sections 3 and 4 come into force on 31 July 2002.** **(2) The rest of this Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.**

## 3 Management of Board

**(1) Section 13(2)(a) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the word “Five”, and substituting the expression “4”.** **(2) Section 13(2)(b) of the principal Act is amended—** (a) by omitting the expression “2”, and substituting the expression “1”:

(b) by omitting the expression “4”, and substituting the expression “2”.

## 4 Schedule 2 amended

Clauses 5(3) and (6)(a) of Schedule 2 of the principal Act are amended by omitting the expression “5” in both cases where it occurs, and substituting in each case the expression “4”.

## 5 Transitional provision

**(1) The term of office for directors representing region 1 (as defined in the Pork Industry Board Regulations 1999) who are elected before 31 July 2002 expires at the close of that date if the term has not expired sooner.** **(2) Subsection (1) applies whether a director is elected before or after the commencement of this section.**
