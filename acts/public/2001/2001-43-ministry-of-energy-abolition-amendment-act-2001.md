# Ministry of Energy (Abolition) Amendment Act 2001

## Note

This Act is administered in the Ministry of Economic Development.

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

**(1) This Act is the Ministry of Energy (Abolition) Amendment Act 2001.** **(2) In this Act, the Ministry of Energy (Abolition) Act 1989 is called “the principal Act”.**

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Purpose of levies

Section 14 of the principal Act is amended by inserting, after paragraph (b), the following paragraph: “(ba) the dissemination of information to assist consumers to choose, and alternate, between competing electricity retailers (as defined in section 2(1) of the Electricity Act 1992); and”.
