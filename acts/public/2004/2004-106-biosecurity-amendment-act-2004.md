# Biosecurity Amendment Act 2004

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

**(1) This Act is the Biosecurity Amendment Act 2004.** **(2) In this Act, the Biosecurity Act 1993 is called “the principal Act”.**

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on 1 January 2005.

## 3 Duty to provide information

**(1) Section 43(1)(a) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the words “or unwanted organisms” where they first occur, and substituting the words “unwanted organisms, or risk goods”.** **(2) Section 43(2)(b) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the words “or organic material”, and substituting the words “, organic material, or risk goods”.**

## 4 Regulations

**(1) Section 165(1)(f) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the word “premises”, and substituting the word “places”.** **(2) Section 165(1)(g) of the principal Act is amended by—** (a) omitting the word “premises” where it first occurs, and substituting the word “places” ; and

(b) omitting the words “, and prescribing technical standards for the construction, equipping, maintenance, and operation of such premises”.

**(3) Section 165(1) of the principal Act is amended by inserting, after paragraph (g), the following paragraph:** “(ga) prescribing technical standards for the construction, equipping, maintenance, and operation of places referred to in paragraph (g) whether required to be registered or not:”

**(4) Section 165(1)(h) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the word “premises”, and substituting the word “places”.** **(5) Section 165(1)(t) of the principal Act is amended by adding the words “or any other chief executive”.**
