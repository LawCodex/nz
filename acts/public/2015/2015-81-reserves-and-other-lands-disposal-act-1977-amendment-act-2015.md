# Reserves and Other Lands Disposal Act 1977 Amendment Act 2015

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the Reserves and Other Lands Disposal Act 1977 Amendment Act 2015.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Purpose of this Act

This Act amends the Reserves and Other Lands Disposal Act 1977 to give effect to clauses 9.43 to 9.47 of the deed of settlement.

## 4 Principal Act

This Act amends the Reserves and Other Lands Disposal Act 1977 (the **principal Act**).

## 5 Section 17 repealed (Te Puna-Topu-O-Hokianga Trust)

Repeal section 17.
