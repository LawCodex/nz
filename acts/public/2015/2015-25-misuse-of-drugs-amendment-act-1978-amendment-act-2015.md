# Misuse of Drugs Amendment Act 1978 Amendment Act 2015

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the Misuse of Drugs Amendment Act 1978 Amendment Act 2015.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Principal Act

This Act amends the Misuse of Drugs Amendment Act 1978 (the **principal Act**).

## 4 Section 10 amended (Interpretation)

In section 10(1), definition of **drug dealing offence**, paragraph (a), delete “46,”.
