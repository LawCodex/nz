# Trade in Endangered Species Amendment Act 2015

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the Trade in Endangered Species Amendment Act 2015.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Principal Act

This Act amends the Trade in Endangered Species Act 1989 (the **principal Act**).

## 4 Section 38 amended (Powers of search)

(1) In section 38(2), replace “on application made in the manner provided in subpart 3 of Part 4 of that Act” with “on application made by an officer in the manner provided in subpart 3 of Part 4 of that Act”.

(2) In section 38(2), replace “may issue a search warrant to that officer” with “may issue a search warrant to every officer named in the warrant”.
