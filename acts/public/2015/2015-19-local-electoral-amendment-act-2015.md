# Local Electoral Amendment Act 2015

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the Local Electoral Amendment Act 2015.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Principal Act

This Act amends the Local Electoral Act 2001 (the **principal Act**).

## 4 Section 19ZC amended (Requirements for valid demand)

In section 19ZC(4), replace “28 February” with “21 February”.

## 5 Section 19ZF amended (Poll of electors)

In section 19ZF(3), replace “82 days” with “89 days”.

## 6 Section 30 amended (Requirements for valid demand)

In section 30(3A), replace “28 February” with “21 February”.

## 7 Section 31 amended (Local authority may resolve to hold poll)

In section 31(1), replace “28 February” with “21 February”.

## 8 Section 33 amended (Poll of electors)

In section 33(3), replace “82 days” with “89 days”.

## 9 Section 102 amended (New election or poll if election or poll declared void)

In section 102(2)(a) and (b), replace “82 days” with “89 days”.

## 10 Section 120 amended (Election to fill extraordinary vacancy)

In section 120(1)(b), replace “82 days” with “89 days”.

## 11 Section 138A amended (Special provision in relation to certain elections to fill extraordinary vacancies and certain polls)

(1) In section 138A(1)(a), replace “10 February” with “17 February”.

(2) In section 138A(1)(b), replace “7 March” with “14 March”.

(3) In section 138A(1)(c), replace “4 April” with “11 April”.
