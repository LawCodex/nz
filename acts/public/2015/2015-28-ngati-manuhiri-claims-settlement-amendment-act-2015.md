# Ngāti Manuhiri Claims Settlement Amendment Act 2015

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the Ngāti Manuhiri Claims Settlement Amendment Act 2015.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Principal Act

This Act amends the Ngāti Manuhiri Claims Settlement Act 2012 (the **principal Act**).

## 4 Section 112 amended (Meaning of RFR land)

In section 112(1)(a)(ii), delete “or a Crown body”.
