# Governor-General Amendment Act 2015

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the Governor-General Amendment Act 2015.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Principal Act

This Act amends the Governor-General Act 2010 (the **principal Act**).

## 4 Section 12 amended (Permanent appropriation for salary, allowance, annuities, etc)

In section 12(a), after “section 5”, insert “and compulsory remuneration-related payments”.
