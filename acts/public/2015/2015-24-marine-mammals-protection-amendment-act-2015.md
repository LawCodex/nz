# Marine Mammals Protection Amendment Act 2015

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the Marine Mammals Protection Amendment Act 2015.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Principal Act

This Act amends the Marine Mammals Protection Act 1978 (the **principal Act**).

## 4 Section 14 amended (Officer may obtain warrant)

In section 14(1), replace “to an officer named in the warrant” with “to every officer named in the warrant”.
