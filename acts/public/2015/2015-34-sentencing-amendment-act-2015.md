# Sentencing Amendment Act 2015

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the Sentencing Amendment Act 2015.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Principal Act

This Act amends the Sentencing Act 2002 (the **principal Act**).

## 4 Section 140A amended (What happens if lessor does not apply to Registrar before motor vehicle sold or disposed of)

In section 140A(5)(b)(i), replace “less than 1 year” with “1 year or less”.
