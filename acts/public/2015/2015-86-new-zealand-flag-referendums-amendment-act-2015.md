# New Zealand Flag Referendums Amendment Act 2015

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the New Zealand Flag Referendums Amendment Act 2015.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## Part 1 Amendments to New Zealand Flag Referendums Act 2015

### 3 Principal Act

This Part amends the New Zealand Flag Referendums Act 2015 (the **principal Act**).

### 4 Section 5 amended (Interpretation)

In section 5, definition of **option**, paragraph (a), replace “4 alternative flag designs” with “5 alternative flag designs”.

### 5 Section 13 amended (Alternative flag designs)

**(1)** In section 13(1), replace “60 days before the commencement of the voting period for the first flag referendum, prescribe 4 alternative flag designs” with “40 days before the commencement of the voting period for the first flag referendum, prescribe 5 alternative flag designs”.

**(2)** In section 13(3), replace “4 alternative flag designs” with “5 alternative flag designs”.

### 6 Schedule 1 replaced

Replace Schedule 1 with the Schedule 1 set out in the Schedule of this Act.
