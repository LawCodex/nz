# Tariff (Free Trade Agreement between New Zealand and the Republic of Korea) Amendment Act 2015

## Note

Changes authorised by subpart 2 of Part 2 of the Legislation Act 2012 have been made in this official reprint.

Note 4 at the end of this reprint provides a list of the amendments incorporated.

This Act is administered by the Ministry of Business, Innovation, and Employment.

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the Tariff (Free Trade Agreement between New Zealand and the Republic of Korea) Amendment Act 2015.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on a date appointed by the Governor-General by Order in Council.

> Section 2 this Act: brought into force, on 20 December 2015, by the Tariff (Free Trade Agreement between New Zealand and the Republic of Korea) Amendment Act 2015 Commencement Order 2015 (LI 2015/318).

## Amendments to Tariff Act 1988

## 3 Principal Act

Sections 4 and 5 amend the Tariff Act 1988 (the **principal Act**).

## 4 Section 15A amended (Interpretation)

**(1)** In section 15A, definition of **free trade agreement**, replace paragraph (d) with:

(d) the Malaysia FTA; or

(e) the Republic of Korea FTA

**(2)** In section 15A, insert in its appropriate alphabetical order:

**Republic of Korea FTA** means the Free Trade Agreement between New Zealand and the Republic of Korea done at Seoul on 23 March 2015

## 5 Section 15H amended (Provisional transitional safeguard measure)

In section 15H(1)(b)(ii), replace “or the Malaysia FTA,” with “the Malaysia FTA, or the Republic of Korea FTA,”.

## Amendments to Tariff

## 6 Tariff amended

Sections 7 and 8, in accordance with section 9F(1) of the principal Act, amend the Tariff.

## 7 Tariff, note 2 amended

In the notes to the Tariff, note 2, penultimate paragraph, after “HK,”, insert “KR,”.

## 8 Tariff, note 3 amended

In the notes to the Tariff, note 3, below the item relating to Hong Kong, China, insert:

|  | Korea, Republic of | KR |
|---|---|---|
