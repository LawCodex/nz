## 5 Board may prohibit or regulate planting of trees along river banks

(1) The Board may from time to time, by special order,—

(a) Prohibit or regulate the planting of any trees, hedges, or other plants or the erection of any structures on or within a specified distance from the banks of any river, stream, watercourse, or drain within the jurisdiction of the Board where they will obstruct or are likely to obstruct the free passage along the banks of the river, stream, watercourse, or drain of machinery or apparatus used for the purposes of cleaning the river, stream, watercourse, or drain:

(b) Require owners and occupiers of land on which any trees, hedges, or other plants are planted or structures are erected in breach of the special order to remove the same.

(2) Every person who commits a breach of any such special order commits an offence, and shall be liable on conviction to a fine not exceeding $20.

Section 5(2): amended, on 1 July 2013, by section 413 of the Criminal Procedure Act 2011 (2011 No 81).
