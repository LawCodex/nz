# Government Superannuation Fund Amendment Act 1997

## Note

This Act is administered by the Ministry of Government Superannuation Fund Department

An Act to amend the Government Superannuation Fund Act 1956

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Government Superannuation Fund Amendment Act 1997, and is part of the Government Superannuation Fund Act 1956 (**the principal Act**).

## 2 Actuarial examinations of Fund

**(1) This subsection substituted s 94(1) of the principal Act.** **(2) The Government Actuary is not required to examine the financial position of the Fund as at 31 March 1997 (as required by section 94(1) of the principal Act before its repeal by subsection (1)).**

## 3 Authority to incur certain expenses

This section inserted, after section 95, s 95AA of the principal Act.
