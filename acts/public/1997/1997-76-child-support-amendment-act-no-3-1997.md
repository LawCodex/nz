# Child Support Amendment Act (No 3) 1997

## Note

This Act is administered in the Inland Revenue Department

An Act to amend the Child Support Act 1991

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Child Support Amendment Act (No 3) 1997, and is part of the Child Support Act 1991 (“the principal Act”).

## 2 Interpretation

**(1) This subsection amended the definition of the term **Commissioner** in s 2(1) of the principal Act.** **(3) This subsection inserted the definition of the term **Officer of the Department** in s 2(1) of the principal Act.**

## 3 Meaning of term **living allowance**

**(1) This subsection amended s 30(5) of the principal Act.** **(2) Subsection (1) is deemed to have come into force on 10 May 1996.**

## 4 Evidence relating to assessments

**(1) This subsection amended s 83(1) of the principal Act.** **(2) This subsection amended s 83(2) of the principal Act.**
