# Copyright Amendment Act 1997

## Note

This Act is administered in the Ministry of Commerce

An Act to amend the Copyright Act 1994

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Copyright Amendment Act 1997, and is part of the Copyright Act 1994 (“the principal Act”).

## 2 New sections substituted

This section substituted ss 137 and 138 of the principal Act.

## 3 Notice of determination

This section substituted s 139(1) of the principal Act.
