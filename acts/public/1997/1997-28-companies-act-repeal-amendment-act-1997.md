# Companies Act Repeal Amendment Act 1997

An Act to amend the Companies Act Repeal Act 1993

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Companies Act Repeal Amendment Act 1997, and is part of the Companies Act Repeal Act 1993 (“the principal Act”).

## 2 Savings

This section inserted s 3(2) to (4) of the principal Act.
