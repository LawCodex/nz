# Child Support Amendment Act (No 2) 1997

## Note

This Act is administered in the Inland Revenue Department

An Act to amend the Child Support Act 1991

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title and commencement

**(1) This Act may be cited as the Child Support Amendment Act (No 2) 1997, and is part of the Child Support Act 1991 (**the principal Act**).** **(2) This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.**

## 2 Savings in respect of outstanding liable parent contributions

This section amended s 256(1)(a) of the principal Act.

## 3 Savings in respect of outstanding maintenance liabilities

(a) This paragraph amended s 259(1)(a) of the principal Act.

(b) This paragraph amended s 259(1)(b) of the principal Act.
