# Companies Act 1993 Amendment Act 1997

An Act to amend the Companies Act 1993

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Companies Act 1993 Amendment Act 1997, and is part of the Companies Act 1993 (“the principal Act”).

## 2 Rights and powers attaching to shares

This section amended section 36(2) of the principal Act.

## 3 Notice of share issue

This section amended section 43(1) of the principal Act.

## 4 Consideration for issue of shares on registration

This section inserted section 46A of the principal Act.

## 5 Redemption on fixed date

This section amended section 75(1)(c) of the principal Act.

## 6 Statement of rights to be given to shareholders

This section substituted section 83(2)(d) of the principal Act.

## 7 Company to satisfy solvency test

This section inserted section 108(5A) of the principal Act.

## 8 Resolution in lieu of meeting

**(1) This subsection substituted section 122(1) of the principal Act.** **(2) This subsection inserted section 122(3A) of the principal Act.**

## 9 Shareholders entitled to receive distributions, attend meetings, and exercise rights

**(1) This subsection substituted section 125(1)(c) of the principal Act.** **(2) This subsection substituted section 125(1)(e) of the principal Act.**

## 10 Major transactions

This section amended section 129(2A) of the principal Act.

## 11 Duty of directors to act in good faith and in best interests of company

This subsection amended section 131(4) of the principal Act.

## 12 Disclosure of interest

This section substituted section 140(1A) of the principal Act.

## 13 Meaning of **relevant interest**

This section amended section 146(2) of the principal Act.

## 14 Method of contracting

This section substituted section 180(1A) of the principal Act.

## 15 Appointment of auditors

This section substituted section 196(2) of the principal Act.

## 16 Short form amalgamation

**(1) This subsection amended s 222(1)(b)(iii) and inserted s 222(1)(b)(iv) of the principal Act.** **(2) This subsection amended s 222(2)(b)(iii) and inserted s 222(2)(b)(iv) of the principal Act.** **(3) This subsection amended s 222(5) of the principal Act.**

## 17 New heading substituted

This section substituted the heading above s 316A of the principal Act.

## 18 Transitional provision in relation to Liquidation Surplus Account under section 290 of Companies Act 1955

This section inserted s 316B of the principal Act.

## 19 Notice of intention to remove where company has ceased to carry on business

This section amended s 319(3)(a) of the principal Act.

## 20 Notice of intention to remove in other cases

Section 320(4)(a) of the principal Act is amended by omitting the words “and its registered office”.

## 21 Registrar may restore company to New Zealand register

This section substituted s 328(3) of the principal Act.

## 22 Registration of documents

This section amended s 362(1)(b) of the principal Act.

## 23 Schedule 5 amended

**(1) This subsection amended clause 5 of Schedule 5 of the principal Act.** **(2) This subsection amended clause 6(4) of Schedule 5 of the principal Act.** **(3) This subsection amended clause 7(4) of Schedule 5 of the principal Act.**
