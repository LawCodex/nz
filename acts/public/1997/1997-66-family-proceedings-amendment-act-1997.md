# Family Proceedings Amendment Act 1997

## Note

This Act is administered in the Ministry of Justice

An Act to amend the Family Proceedings Act 1980

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title and commencement

**(1) This Act may be cited as the Family Proceedings Amendment Act 1997, and is part of the Family Proceedings Act 1980 (“the principal Act”).** **(2) This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.**

## 2 Power to issue deduction notices under Social Security Act 1964

This section inserted s 101B to the principal Act.
