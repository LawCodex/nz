# Tax Administration Amendment Act 1997

## Note

This Act is administered in the Inland Revenue Department

An Act to amend the Tax Administration Act 1994

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title and commencement

**(1) This Act may be cited as the Tax Administration Amendment Act 1997, and is part of the Tax Administration Act 1994 (“the principal Act”).** **(2) This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.**

## 2 Disclosure of address information in relation to debtors

(a) This paragraph amended para (b) of the definition of the term **debtor** in s 85(6) of the principal Act.

(b) This paragraph inserted para (c) of the definition of the term **debtor** in s 85(6) of the principal Act.
