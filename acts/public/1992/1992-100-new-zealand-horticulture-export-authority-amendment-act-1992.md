# New Zealand Horticulture Export Authority Amendment Act 1992

## Note

This Act is administered in the Ministry of Agriculture

An Act to amend the New Zealand Horticulture Export Authority Act 1987

BE IT ENACTED by the Parliament of New Zealand as follows:

## 1 Short Title

This Act may be cited as the New Zealand Horticulture Export Authority Amendment Act 1992, and shall be read together with and deemed part of the New Zealand Horticulture Export Authority Act 1987 (hereinafter referred to as the principal Act).
