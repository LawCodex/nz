# Civil Defence Emergency Management Amendment Act 2017

The Parliament of New Zealand enacts as follows:

## 1 Title

This Act is the Civil Defence Emergency Management Amendment Act 2017.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Principal Act

This Act amends the Civil Defence Emergency Management Act 2002 (the **principal Act**).

## 4 Schedule 1AA amended

In Schedule 1AA, after clause 7, insert the Part 2 set out in the Schedule of this Act.

## Schedule New Part 2 inserted in Schedule 1AA

s 4

### Part 2 Provision relating to Civil Defence Emergency Management Amendment Act 2017

#### 8 Continuation of national civil defence emergency management strategy to 2019

Despite section 34(1)(b), the national civil defence emergency management strategy that took effect on 10 January 2008, unless it is earlier amended or replaced, remains current (in effect) until the close of 9 April 2019.
