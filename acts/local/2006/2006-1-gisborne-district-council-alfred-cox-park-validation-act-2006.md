# Gisborne District Council (Alfred Cox Park) Validation Act 2006

## Note

Changes authorised by subpart 2 of Part 2 of the Legislation Act 2012 have been made in this official reprint.

Note 4 at the end of this reprint provides a list of the amendments incorporated.

## 1 Title

This Act is the Gisborne District Council (Alfred Cox Park) Validation Act 2006.

## 2 Commencement

This Act comes into force on the day after the date on which it receives the Royal assent.

## 3 Interpretation

In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires,—

**Council** means the Gisborne District Council

**Alfred Cox Park** means the land described in the Schedule.

## 4 Certain uses of Alfred Cox Park validated

**(1)** The use of Alfred Cox Park, and the actions of the Council in permitting the use of Alfred Cox Park, as a site for a flea market and a visitor information centre are deemed to be and to have always been lawful.

**(2)** Subsection (1) overrides—

(a) the Reserves Act 1977; and

(b) the deed of trust, dated 16 May 1944, between Alfred James Cox and the Mayor, Councillors, and Burgesses of the Borough of Gisborne.

## 5 Application of Reserves Act 1977

**(1)** The Reserves Act 1977 applies to Alfred Cox Park, subject to this Act.

**(2)** This section applies to avoid doubt.

## Schedule Alfred Cox Park

s 3

All those pieces of land containing 2.0851 hectares more or less being lots 106-110 on Deposited Plan 1357 lot 1 on Deposited Plan 2749 and lot 1 on Deposited Plan 3627 being all of the land comprised in record of title volume 2A folio 485 Gisborne Registry.

> Schedule: amended, on 12 November 2018, by section 250 of the Land Transfer Act 2017 (2017 No 30).
