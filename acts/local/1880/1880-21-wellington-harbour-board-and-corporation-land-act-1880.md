# Wellington Harbour Board and Corporation Land Act 1880

An Act to vest certain Land in the Wellington Harbour Board, and also to vest certain other Land in the Mayor, Councillors, and Citizens of the City of Wellington.

BE IT ENACTED by the General Assembly of New Zealand in Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows:—

## 1 Short Title

The Short Title of this Act is The Wellington Harbour Board and Corporation Land Act 1880.

## 2 Interpretation

In this Act if not inconsistent with the context,— **Board** means the Wellington Harbour Board as constituted under The Wellington Harbour Board Act 1879;

**Minister** means the Minister appointed under or acting for such under The Public Works Act 1876.

## 3 Provisions of The Harbours Act 1878, to apply to all lands vested in the Board by this Act

Except where provision is made to the contrary by this Act, all the provisions of The Harbours Act 1878, shall extend to all the lands and the wharf hereby vested in the Board; and this Act shall be deemed to be a special Act within the meaning of The Harbours Act 1878.

## 4 Land and wharf vested in Board

The parcels of land together with the Railway Wharf described in Schedule 1 to this Act are hereby absolutely vested in the Board, and shall be held by it for the purposes for which the said Board is constituted, and under and subject to the following terms and conditions:—

**(1) The Board shall maintain the Railway Wharf and the breastwork along Waterloo Quay, and all approaches thereto, in good repair and condition in all respects:** **(2) \[Repealed\]** **(3) Her Majesty the Queen may from time to time lay such lines of railway along Waterloo Quay and the Railway Wharf, and the approaches thereto, as may be thought fit, and may from time to time remove and alter the same:** **(4) No compensation or allowance shall be made or paid by Her said Majesty to the Board for the use or occupation of the Railway Wharf, or Waterloo Quay, or any of the land hereby vested in the Board:** **(5) The lines of railway built on the said wharf and Waterloo Quay shall be maintained by Her Majesty the Queen, who may conduct the whole of the traffic on the said railway and Railway Wharf, under and subject to any Act, by-laws, or regulations for the time being in force, and applicable generally to railways and works connected therewith.** Subsection (2) was repealed, as from 16 September 1938, by section 38(2)(a) Reserves and Other Lands Disposal Act 1938 (1938 No 19).

## 5 Restriction on powers of Board in respect of lands in Schedule 1

The Board shall not have any power to sell, mortgage, charge, lease, or otherwise alienate or dispose of the land and wharf referred to in the said Schedule 1, or any right or interest therein; nor shall the said land or the Railway Wharf or any such right or interest be liable to be dealt with or in any way affected by or under the provisions of sections 208 to 211 inclusive of The Harbours Act 1878.

## 6 Board not to execute works without sanction of Minister

The Board shall not construct any buildings or execute any works on Waterloo Quay, or on the piece of land adjoining the railway or the Railway Wharf, without having first obtained the consent in writing of the Minister.

## 7 Powers of Minister in respect of land and wharf mentioned in Schedule 1

Should the Board fail or neglect to perform or observe any condition by this Act imposed upon it with respect to the land and wharf described in the said Schedule 1, or which, in the opinion of the Minister, are necessary to insure the safety of Her Majesty's subjects, or of any of Her Majesty's property, the Minister may, after having given notice in writing of his intention to the Board, forthwith do all acts and things necessary for giving effect to this provision; and the cost of so doing shall be recoverable from the Board as a debt due to the Crown, or may be deducted from any moneys due or payable to the Board by or on behalf of Her Majesty or the Government of the colony.

## 8 Endowments for Board

The pieces of land described in Schedule 2 to this Act are hereby vested in the Board as an endowment for the said Board, and shall be held by them subject to the provisions of The Harbours Act 1878.

## 9 Land vested in Corporation of Wellington

The piece of land described in Schedule 3 to this Act is hereby vested in the Mayor, Councillors, and Citizens of the City of Wellington, for the like purposes and objects as are set forth in The Te Aro Reclamation Act 1879; and the provisions of that Act shall apply and extend to the said piece of land in the same manner as if it had been included within the lands affected thereby.
