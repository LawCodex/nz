# Patea Borough Council Empowering Act 1930

An Act to confer Additional Powers on the Patea Borough Council by way of leasing and selling certain Lands, and to validate certain Leases already granted.

BE IT ENACTED by the General Assembly of New Zealand in Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows:—

## 1 Short Title

This Act may be cited as the Patea Borough Council Empowering Act 1930.

## 2 Interpretation

In this Act, if not inconsistent with the context,— **Corporation** means the Corporation of the Mayor, Councillors, and Burgesses of the Borough of Patea

**Council** means the Patea Borough Council.

## 3 Leasing

Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in the Reserves Act 1977, the Council may exercise in respect of the lands comprised in Schedule 1 hereto all the powers of leasing conferred on it by the Local Government Act 1974, with respect to lands held by it for municipal purposes under that Act and all the powers of leasing conferred on it as a leasing authority under the Public Bodies Leases Act 1969. A reference to the Reserves and Domains Act 1953 was substituted, as from 1 April 1954, for a reference to the Public Reserves, Domains, and National Parks Act 1928 pursuant to section 107(1) Reserves and Domains Act 1953 (1953 No 69). That reference was in turn substituted, as from 1 April 1978, by a reference to the Reserves Act 1977 (1977 No 66) pursuant to section 125(1) Reserves Act 1977 (1977 No 66).

A reference to the Municipal Corporations Act 1933 was substituted, as from 20 December 1933, for a reference to the Municipal Corporations Act 1920 pursuant to section 393(1) Municipal Corporations Act 1933 (1933 No 30). That reference was in turn substituted, as from 1 October 1954, by a reference to the Municipal Corporations Act 1954 pursuant to section 413 Municipal Corporations Act 1954 (1954 No 76). That reference was in turn substituted, as from 1 April 1980, by a reference to the Local Government Act 1974 pursuant to section 9(1) Local Government Amendment Act 1979 (1979 No 59).

The reference to the Public Bodies Leases Act 1969 was substituted, as from 1 January 1970, for a reference to the Public Bodies Leases Act 1908 pursuant to section 28 Public Bodies Leases Act 1969 (1969 No 141).

## 4 Selling-powers

**(1) Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in any Act, the Council may sell any of the lands comprised in Schedule 2 hereto or any part or parts thereof at such time or times and at such prices and upon such terms and with such rights of way over adjoining lands vested in the Corporation as it may think fit, and on the sale of any such land any trust or reservation theretofore affecting the same shall be deemed to be cancelled.** **(2) The net amount received from the sale of any parcel of land as hereby authorized shall be paid into a capital fund, to be called the Reserves Realization Fund, and the moneys belonging to such fund shall be invested in the same manner as moneys belonging to reserve funds established by Borough Councils in respect of trading undertakings may be invested, and the income arising therefrom shall be applied towards relief of rates.**

## 5 Leases validated

**(1) The leases more particularly referred to in Schedule 3 hereto and the respective registrations thereof in the Land Registry Office at New Plymouth are hereby declared to be valid and effectual.** **(2) Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in the Reserves Act 1977, the Council, with respect to the several leases mentioned in Schedule 3 hereto, may exercise any of the powers conferred on it by sections one hundred and fifty-eight, one hundred and fifty-nine, and one hundred and sixty of the Municipal Corporations Act 1920, with respect to lands held by it for municipal purposes under that Act or conferred on it as a leasing authority by section twelve of the Public Bodies' Leases Act 1908.** A reference to the Reserves and Domains Act 1953 in subsection (2) was substituted, as from 1 April 1954, for a reference to the Public Reserves, Domains, and National Parks Act 1928 pursuant to section 107(1) Reserves and Domains Act 1953 (1953 No 69). That reference was in turn substituted, as from 1 April 1978, by a reference to the Reserves Act 1977 (1977 No 66) pursuant to section 125(1) Reserves Act 1977 (1977 No 66).

## 6 Defining leasing-powers

The powers of leasing and of dealing with lands hereby conferred shall be in addition to, and not in substitution for, the powers of leasing conferred by the Reserves Act 1977. A reference to the Reserves and Domains Act 1953 was substituted, as from 1 April 1954, for a reference to the Public Reserves, Domains, and National Parks Act 1928 pursuant to section 107(1) Reserves and Domains Act 1953 (1953 No 69). That reference was in turn substituted, as from 1 April 1978, by a reference to the Reserves Act 1977 (1977 No 66) pursuant to section 125(1) Reserves Act 1977 (1977 No 66).

## 7 Authorizing lease to Royal New Zealand Society for Health of Women and Children

Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in the Reserves Act 1977, the Council is hereby authorized to grant to the Patea Branch of the Royal New Zealand Society for the Health of Women and Children (Incorporated) a lease of all that parcel of land comprised in Schedule 4 hereto, or such part thereof as the Council may determine, for a term of nine hundred and ninety-nine years at a peppercorn rent, and upon such terms and conditions as the Council may determine, but including a provision that the Council shall have the right to determine the lease and take full control and possession of the premises thereon should such premises at any time cease to be used for the purpose for which they have been erected—namely, as Plunket rooms—or should the Patea Branch of the Royal New Zealand Society for the Health of Women and Children (Incorporated) become defunct. A reference to the Reserves and Domains Act 1953 was substituted, as from 1 April 1954, for a reference to the Public Reserves, Domains, and National Parks Act 1928 pursuant to section 107(1) Reserves and Domains Act 1953 (1953 No 69). That reference was in turn substituted, as from 1 April 1978, by a reference to the Reserves Act 1977 (1977 No 66) pursuant to section 125(1) Reserves Act 1977 (1977 No 66).
