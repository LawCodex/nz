# Church Trust Property at Little River Exchange Act 1878

An Act to amend The Church Trust Property at Little River Exchange Act 1877.

## Preamble

WHEREAS it is expedient to amend The Church Trust Property at Little River Exchange Act 1877 (hereinafter called **the said Act**), by substituting the words “eighteen months” for the words “six months” in the second section of the said Act:

BE IT THEREFORE ENACTED by the General Assembly of New Zealand in Parliament assembled, and by the authority of the same, as follows:—

## 1 Short Title

The Short Title of this Act shall be The Church Trust Property at Little River Exchange Act 1877 Amendment Act, 1878.

## 2 “Eighteen” substituted for “six”

Section two of the said Act shall be read as if the words “eighteen months” had been inserted instead of the words “six months”, where they occur in the said section.
